Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2025

At Bin Azeez Perfumes, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Full name and contact number
  • Delivery address (city, province)
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Order details and payment method

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To contact you about your order status
  • To send you updates and promotional offers (only if you agree)
  • To improve our products and services

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Courier/delivery partners to fulfil your order
  • When required by law

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data. Our website uses secure connections and your information is only accessible to authorised staff involved in order processing.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies. We only use essential session data to keep your cart working during your visit.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data
  • Opt out of any marketing communications

7. Returns & Refunds

We offer a 14-day return policy. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 14 days of delivery. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
